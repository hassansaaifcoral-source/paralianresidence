Our Story

About Paralian

Who We Are

A Place to Feel at Home

Paralian was born from a simple belief: that a great stay should feel effortless. Nestled in the growing city of Hulhumalé — just minutes from Velana International Airport — we offer boutique hotel rooms and fully serviced residences to travellers and long-term guests alike.

Our name, Paralian, refers to a person who lives by the sea — and that spirit runs through everything we do. The ocean isn't just a backdrop here; it's part of the experience. Every room, every dish at the café, every curated activity is designed with the Maldivian sea and sky in mind.

We are a Maldivian property, proudly run by a local team that brings genuine warmth, island knowledge, and meticulous care to every guest's stay.

From the Airport

We are 2km from Velana International Airport (MLE). A taxi or bus from the airport to Hulhumalé takes 15–20 minutes via the Sinamalé Bridge. We offer arranged transfers on request.

⛴️
From Malé City

Regular public ferry service connects Malé to Hulhumalé ferry terminal. Journey time: approx. 15 minutes, fare: MVR 10.

🚌
Local Transport

Taxis and ride-sharing apps operate on the island. The hotel can arrange on-island transport for guests.
