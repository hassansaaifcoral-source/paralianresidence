Paralian Café

About the Café

Island Flavours,
Rooftop Views

Perched atop Paralian, our café is the perfect vantage point for Hulhumalé's horizon. Whether you're starting your morning with freshly brewed coffee and eggs, sharing an afternoon bite, or watching the sunset with a cold drink in hand — the Paralian Café is your island retreat.

Our menu features local Maldivian dishes alongside international favourites, crafted with fresh, sustainably sourced ingredients. Open to hotel guests and drop-in visitors alike.
