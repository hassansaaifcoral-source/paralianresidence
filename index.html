About the Project

A New Chapter
in Malé Living

Paralian Residence brings the calm and character of the Paralian brand into the heart of Malé. A thoughtfully designed multi-floor residential building offering fully furnished apartments for long-term residents, short-stay guests, and business travellers.

Every apartment is curated with the same attention to quality that defines our Hulhumalé property — refined materials, smart layouts, and that unmistakable coastal ease.
