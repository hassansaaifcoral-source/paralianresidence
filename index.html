General Questions
+What is included in the long-term lease?
Your lease includes fully furnished accommodation, high-speed Wi-Fi, air conditioning, weekly cleaning, and access to 24/7 support. Utility packages can be arranged separately or bundled into your monthly rate.
+What is the minimum lease period?
Our minimum lease period is 6 months. We offer discounts for 12-month commitments. Shorter durations may be considered on a case-by-case basis.
+Can I schedule a viewing before signing?
Absolutely — and we encourage it. Use the Apartments tab to book a private viewing. We offer morning, afternoon, and weekend slots.
Billing & Payment
+How is rent paid?
Rent is paid monthly via BML bank transfer or BML Payment Gateway. We also accept Visa, Mastercard, and American Express. A security deposit equal to one month's rent is required on signing.
+Are utilities included?
Wi-Fi is always included. Electricity and water can be included in a bundled package or billed separately based on usage — we'll discuss the options that work best for you.
Services & Support
+How often is cleaning provided?
Standard weekly cleaning is included in all long-term leases. Additional cleans, deep-cleans, and linen changes can be requested through the Services tab at any time.
+How quickly will maintenance requests be handled?
Non-urgent requests are typically handled within 24–48 hours. Urgent issues (leaks, electrical, AC failure) are prioritised and attended to the same day.
Policies & Rules
+What is the cancellation / early exit policy?
Early termination requires 30 days written notice. If exiting before the minimum term, a fee equivalent to one month's rent applies. We are flexible and always willing to discuss individual circumstances.
+Are pets and smoking allowed?
Smoking is not permitted inside any apartment or common area. Pets are not currently permitted, though exceptions may be considered — please contact us to discuss.

Short-Stay FAQ

Your Questions Answered

+What is the minimum stay?
Our minimum stay is 2 nights. Weekly and monthly bookings attract discounts of 10% and 20% respectively.
+Is daily housekeeping included?
Yes — all short-stay bookings include daily housekeeping, fresh linen, and toiletry replenishment at no extra charge.
+What is the check-in process?
Self check-in is available from 2:00 PM. You will receive a digital key code before arrival. Our team is available 24/7 via WhatsApp for assistance.
+Can I cancel my booking?
Bookings cancelled 48 hours before check-in receive a full refund. Within 48 hours, the first night is non-refundable. Monthly bookings require 7 days notice.

Daily Rental FAQ

Quick Answers

+Can I book for the same day?
Yes — same-day bookings are welcome, subject to availability. Contact us via WhatsApp for the fastest confirmation. We typically respond within 30 minutes.
+What time is check-in and check-out?
Standard check-in is 2:00 PM and check-out is 12:00 PM (noon). Early check-in from 10 AM and late check-out until 3 PM are available on request, subject to availability.
+Is airport transfer available?
Yes — we can arrange airport transfers to and from Malé Airport (MLE) for USD 15. Please mention it when booking or WhatsApp us your flight details.
+What is included in the daily rate?
All daily rates include high-speed Wi-Fi, air conditioning, fresh linen, towels, a welcome kit, and access to 24/7 WhatsApp support. Daily housekeeping is available on request.
